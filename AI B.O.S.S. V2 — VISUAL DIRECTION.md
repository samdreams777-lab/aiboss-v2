# AI B.O.S.S. V2 — VISUAL DIRECTION

**Status:** Design direction  
**Purpose:** создать визуальную концепцию, которую затем можно передать в техническое implementation task.

---

# 1. CORE CONCEPT

# YOUR BUSINESS.
# CONNECTED.

AI B.O.S.S. должен восприниматься не как web agency и не как generic AI SaaS.

Он должен восприниматься как:

> **digital operating system that turns separate business touchpoints into one connected experience.**

# 16. VISUAL LANGUAGE

## Typography

Large editorial headlines.

Short supporting text.

Strong numerical hierarchy.

---

## Cards

Large media.

Minimal borders.

Depth through spacing and motion.

---

## Motion

Purposeful.

Scroll-driven.

Progressive.

Smooth.

No constant animation.

---

## Color

Preserve recognizable AI B.O.S.S. identity where possible.

Use contrast and controlled accent color.

Do not introduce unnecessary rainbow gradients or generic “AI neon” aesthetics.

---

# 17. MOBILE

Mobile is not a reduced desktop version.

Business showcase:

horizontal swipe.

Journey:

vertical transformation.

Product demos:

single focused interface.

Typography:

responsive clamp-based scaling.

Animation:

reduced complexity where required.

Touch targets:

minimum practical touch size.

No horizontal overflow.

---

# 18. PERFORMANCE

All visual effects must respect production constraints.

Preferred:

- CSS transforms;
- opacity;
- SVG;
- optimized WebP/AVIF;
- lazy-loaded secondary media;
- GPU-friendly animation.

Avoid:

- unnecessary full-page WebGL;
- huge background videos;
- animation of layout dimensions;
- excessive DOM effects;
- blocking media.

---

# 19. DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The website itself must be the first AI B.O.S.S. demonstration.

The visitor should experience:

**discover → explore → interact → understand → imagine → configure → contact**

without needing to read a long explanation.

---

# 20. SUCCESS CRITERIA

A redesign is successful if a first-time visitor can answer these questions without assistance:

### What is AI B.O.S.S.?

A digital system connecting the customer journey and business touchpoints.

### What do you actually build?

Websites, QR menus, AI interfaces, automation, analytics, loyalty and connected digital experiences.

### Is there proof?

Yes — multiple working prototypes/digital experiences.

### Can this be made for my business?

Yes.

### What should I do next?

Explore a business type or request a custom build/audit.

---

# 21. FINAL DESIGN TEST

Show the finished site to someone unfamiliar with AI B.O.S.S.

Give them only this instruction:

> “Look at this website for 30 seconds.”

Then ask:

**What do you think these people build?**

If the answer is:

> “They build digital systems/web experiences for businesses.”

PASS.

If the answer is:

> “Some kind of AI consulting company.”

FAIL.

If the answer is:

> “I want something like that for my café/restaurant/business.”

EXCELLENT.
